Research method

From public corpus to Fan Index

The study uses purposive sampling of public conversations centered on the greatest-driver question. Its unit of analysis is one explicit preference per anonymized author, with a documented corpus and coding protocol.

01 / CORPUS

Sampling frame

Select public threads and videos whose central prompt asks, ranks, or debates the greatest Formula 1 driver of all time. Record URL, platform, date, and collection limit for every source.

02 / UNIT

Anonymized respondents

Hash author identifiers at collection time, collapse repeated answers, and remove conflicting choices. Names, profile images, and account links are excluded from the research dataset.

03 / CODING

Predefined decision rules

Apply an auditable multilingual codebook to direct endorsements, first-place rankings, and short prompt answers. Ambiguous mentions, negations, and relative comparisons are excluded.

04 / SYNTHESIS

Source-level estimates

Calculate driver shares in conversations with at least five coded choices, consolidate them into the Fan Index, and publish raw counts, source coverage, and uncertainty intervals alongside the result.
