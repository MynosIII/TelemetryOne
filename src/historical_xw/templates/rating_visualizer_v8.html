The shape of greatness

These records use the complete supplied history and ranking files, regardless of which drivers are currently plotted. This page does not invoke or alter a rating model.

How the publishable records are defined

Current driver indexThe post-event ELO rank among drivers who participated in that Grand Prix. It is recalculated at every event.

Career curve areaThe race-by-race integral of ELO above or below that event's field median. Multi-season retirement gaps are cut, not bridged. Drivers need 25 events.

Lowest-rating recordsCareer and final ELO come from the supplied ranking. The single-race low is the minimum point visible in the full history for drivers with at least five starts.

Best carThe highest season-average v6 expected-car-win value for an identified car model with at least four events. It measures model expectation, not championship points.
